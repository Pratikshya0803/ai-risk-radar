How the score works

Transparent, evidence-based monitoring rather than a claim of absolute safety.

The Radar score starts at 100 and deducts 15 points for each verified high-severity event, 6 for medium severity, and 2 for low severity. Multiple events accumulate, with the score capped at 0. A platform with no verified events is shown as Insufficient verified data rather than receiving a misleading perfect score. This follows the general risk-assessment principle of separating severity from likelihood and documenting the evidence behind assessments. The score is a monitoring indicator, not a certification or prediction.
